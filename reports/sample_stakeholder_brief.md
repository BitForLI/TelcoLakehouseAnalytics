# Sample Customer Retention Brief

Snapshot date: **2026-09-02**

## Executive view

- The snapshot contains **7,043 customers** with an overall churn rate of **26.5%**.
- Current monthly revenue is **$456,116.60**. Customers marked as churned account for
  **$139,130.85** of that monthly revenue.
- The highest observed churn rate among sufficiently sized segments is
  **tenure band = 00–12 months** at **47.4%** (1,037 of 2,186 customers).
- The largest monthly revenue-at-risk segment is **contract = Month-to-month** at
  **$120,847.10**.

## Recommended next analysis

- Month-to-month customers have a **42.7%** churn rate, compared with **6.8%** across one- and
  two-year contracts. Test whether an early-tenure retention offer changes this gap.
- Start with the month-to-month segment because it combines material revenue exposure with a
  clearly defined customer group.
- Treat these findings as associations rather than proof of causation. Measure any intervention
  with a controlled experiment before expanding it.
